About Me

From the lab bench to the written page.

Bridging the Gap Between Science and Society

With a Ph.D. in Molecular Biology and over a decade of research experience, I seamlessly transition complex data into compelling narratives that captivate both experts and general audiences alike.

My writing has been featured in top-tier journals, technology magazines, and mainstream media outlets. I specialize in breaking down intricate subjects—from CRISPR gene editing and quantum computing to the latest discoveries from the James Webb Space Telescope—ensuring the science remains accurate while telling a deeply human story.

Beyond writing, I frequently consult with research institutions to help scientists communicate their findings effectively to the public and policymakers. My goal is to combat misinformation and foster an enduring curiosity about the world we live in.
